Haven Rally v1.2

Arcade racer in this browser. Two live chassis, three circuit highways, an endless start-to-finish run with traffic and guns, and a real drag strip. Ghost of your best heat. Local ledger. No account. This page is the spec the live client follows. RESOURCE on the public page; dual ledgers / Star Chart stay CANON.

1. Loop

Circuits. Countdown 3–2–1–GO (Options can skip). Full heats: Pine Coil 24 laps, Coral Coast 20, Singularity Ring 30. After the track pick: Solo ghost, 2P split-screen, vs AI (Reed / Mira / Kai), or 2P+AI. Pass sector gantries, then the start line. Slide charges boost. Beat the ghost in Solo for this circuit + craft.

Endless. One seeded start-to-finish highway, ~28,000 yd (~16 mi), four lanes, checkpoints, tunnel, FINISH gantry. Light traffic as obstacles. Right Shift spends the boost bar for speed and front guns; empty bar fires nothing. Wrecks sip boost, add score, and raise an uncapped combo. Each combo point is +5 mph top speed. Ram a car or leave the asphalt and the chain (and the bonus speed) dumps. Boost trickles slowly on-track; drift still charges it. Original announcer vocals fire on the streak ladder. High score stays in this browser.

Drag. Stage, READY, sportsman Christmas tree, AI in an inner lane. Trap speed and ET write to the local hall.

3. Physics (math first)

World yards; engine SI. Every craft is craftNorm’d so new cars only need stats. Apex Mk I is the GT baseline; Boxcut Mk I is the short-box truck.

Engine. Torque curve (N·m) peaks at tqRpm from torque lb-ft. Fuel cut at redline. Wheel force T × gear × final × η / r. Aero top speed v³ = P / (½ ρ Cd A), plus combo bonus on Endless.

Gears (launch pack). Shared six-speed [3.28, 2.08, 1.48, 1.16, 0.97, 0.84]. 1–2–3 are the hole-shot: tall enough to stay in, extra drive so they slingshot (1st can light the rears; 2nd/3rd hook). Upshift ~96.5% redline in those three, with a small speed kick on the 1→2 and 2→3. 4th-on is the boat: softer torque, earlier shift (~91.5% redline), speed piles on instead of launching. Clutch open ~0.09–0.14 s on a shift. Downshift when RPM would sit under the torque peak.

Drive. rwd / fwd / awd sets which axle gets torque. Traction μ N_driven with weight shift. If drive force exceeds that, wheelSlip rises: RWD yaws (spin), FWD pushes wide, AWD holds longer. Future HP upgrades without extra μ will spin.

Brake / slide. brakeMu × mass × g × 0.72. E-brake is extra rear μ and a slide. Lateral: vh chases heading at a rate from μ. On-track slip charges boost; Right Shift spends it. Off-track is time-scaled dump, not a per-frame kill.

Boost. Bar 0…boostTank (Apex 1.00, Boxcut 1.42). Drain is the same rate, so the truck’s bar lasts longer. Hit formula: extra torque 0.32 × 1.1 × boostPower while Right Shift is down and fuel remains. Boxcut boostPower = 1.25 (25% harder shove than Apex). Endless wrecks add ~0.11 tank. On-road trickle is slow on Endless, faster on circuits.

Nearest-segment project onto the densified path gives along-track s and signed lateral. |lat| > width is off-track. Every course is four lanes: half-width 2 × 4.4 yd, painted dividers, yellow edges. Sectors fire when s crosses their fraction.

6. Endless arcade

~24–64 traffic cars on the highway (dense in the first stretch). Ram dumps speed and breaks combo. Guns (Right Shift + fuel) hitscan ~52 yd ahead; wrecks are 100 × combo points. Combo has no cap. Each point adds 5 mph to top speed (aero drag scaled so the extra speed is reachable). Wall or ram: COMBO BREAK, bonus speed gone. Timeouts do not break the chain.

7. Ghost + ledger

Each heat records {x,y,h,t} samples. Best finish time per circuit|craft keeps the ghost. Endless also writes save.arcade: bestScore, bestCombo, last 20 wreck-runs. Ledger key lygo-haven-rally-v1 in localStorage. RESOURCE on the public page; this browser is the hall. No fake global board.

8. Studio + presentation

Title screen matches Lattice Golf: full-bleed still of the selected craft, right panel, operator name, garage chip, mode cards, radio, PayPal.me/ExcavationPro + Patreon. Garage is a live 3D studio (drag to orbit) with two bays. 2.5D Three.js follow-cam; 2D top-down fallback if WebGL fails.

Cameras (C or Options): Chase, Close, Hood, Bumper, Cockpit, TV. Chase distance/height sliders scale Chase and TV.

FX. Headlights and brake lamps. First-gear launch lays black burnout marks and spins the rears. Right Shift with fuel: tailpipe flames. Reduce-effects hides sparks, gun tracers, extra lights, and new skids.

HUD: commercial race cluster bottom-right (tach, gear, mph or km/h). Endless adds score, combo, wrecks, browser best.
