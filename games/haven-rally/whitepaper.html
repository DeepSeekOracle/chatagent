Haven Rally v1.1

Time-trial racer. One craft, one closed circuit, a ghost of your best run. No account. Local ledger. This page is the spec the live client follows.

1. Loop

Countdown 3–2–1–GO. Circuits: three laps. Pass sector gates, then the start line. Endless run is one start-to-finish highway (about 2 miles: straights, esses, hairpins, a tunnel, FINISH gantry). Holding a slide charges boost. Beat the ghost. Post the card to this browser.

3. Physics (math first)

World yards; engine SI. Every craft is craftNorm’d so new cars only need stats. Apex Mk I is the baseline.

Engine. Torque curve (N·m) peaks at tqRpm from torque lb-ft. Fuel cut at redline. Wheel force T × gear × final × η / r. Top speed is aero: v³ = P / (½ ρ Cd A).

Gears. RPM locked to road speed × ratio except during a 0.12 s shift (clutch open). Auto upshift ~93% redline, downshift when RPM would sit under the torque peak.

Drive. rwd / fwd / awd sets which axle gets torque. Traction μ N_driven with weight shift. If drive force exceeds that, wheelSlip rises: RWD yaws (spin), FWD pushes wide, AWD holds longer. Future HP upgrades without extra μ will spin.

Brake. brakeMu × mass × g × 0.72. E-brake is extra rear μ and a slide. Lateral: vh chases heading at a rate from μ. On-track slip charges boost; Right Shift spends it.

Nearest-segment project onto the densified loop gives along-track s and signed lateral. |lat| > width is off-track. Every course is three lanes: half-width 1.5 × 4.4 yd, painted dividers at ±½ lane. Sectors fire when s crosses their fraction.

Drag is a linear strip, not a closed loop. Sportsman tree: pre-stage, stage, three ambers at 0.5 s, then green. Leave before green is a red-light foul. F restages both cars on the line and starts the tree. AI launches on its reaction time (rare red-light).
