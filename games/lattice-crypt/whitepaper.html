1. Loop

Campaign — First Descent is 24 hand-built floors across eight realms. Every third floor is a seal: the cyan exit stays dim until every nexus on that floor is smashed. Difficulty steps: more nexuses, higher rank, blight, gates, thieves, then the Drain. Finish the 24th floor and the lock opens — you may walk into Endless from there.

Endless is the old forever-crypt: seeded rooms, treasure every eighth, rank that keeps climbing, no last floor. The hall ranks score per credit.

Survival is one huge crypt (no exit). Waves pour from off-screen every 28 seconds. Kills grant XP; each level offers three stacking upgrades (Might, Haste, Iron, Heart, Core, Pierce, Volley, Pull, Stride, Resonance, a new arm, or a melee auto: Cleave / Orbit / Aura). Every armed weapon fires at once; picking the same arm again stacks it (damage, bolts, pierce, blades, radius). Fodder HP ≈ 1 + 1.15×wave; player shot = job shot + cores + Might + weapon stacks. A named guardian arrives every five waves. Auto-shoot starts on. Hall score posts as a run with wave written in the floor field.

Health ticks down every second. Smash nexuses. Keys open doors. Vials are smart bombs. Insert a credit (Space / Start) when a warden falls. Auto-shoot (menu tick or L in play) fires for you; aim still follows the last move.

4. Relics

  • Ration +100 health. Flask same, but a stray shot ruins it.
  • Blight looks like a flask — −100 and strips a buff.
  • Key opens one door. Doors also yawn open if the floor stays quiet long enough.
  • Chest score. Vial stored magic. Shooting a vial on the floor is a weaker room-clear.
  • Weapons (Q to cycle) — Shard default bolt. Fan three-way. Needle pierces. Cinder leaves fire. Comet lobs over walls. Halo orbiting wards plus a bolt.
  • Core / Heart / Iron / Phial grow the run: shot power, max health, armor, extra vials. Seals gift one.
  • Shot Codex / Swift / Aegis timed buffs (fire rate, speed, armor).
  • Veil foes lose the warden unless they bump. Reflect shots bounce once; bumpers take damage.
  • Pulse freezes nearby foes. Warp relocates the warden. Gates teleport between paired pads.
  • Trap tiles stun. Exit is the only way down, unless the floor stalls into a wall-exit after three minutes.

5. Spells (vials)

K / Magic / pad B: spend one vial. Damage in view = 20 × magic × Faith scale. Clears fodder, wounds elites, deletes the Drain. Each job fires its named special at the same time (Rend, Cover, Dash, Hymn on shots, Shockwave, Doublespeak, Right-time, Fair Plate, Chorus Flask, Δ9 Seal). Floor vials that get shot do half of that.

Hidden vials on some floors (banner: “A hidden vial waits”). Treasure rush floors (every 8th) are 30 seconds, extra chests, exit in a random corner. Layouts cycle: rooms, cross, cells, halls, arena, ring, vault, spiral.

6. Realms and campaign

Eight zones, each with its own floor, wall-top, wall-face, and deco tiles: Stone flagstone, Frost rime, Ember basalt, Root earth, Tide wet stone, Gold marble, Void obsidian, Lattice metal. Walls autotile — interior brick is dark void, edges show a face — so walkable ground is obvious. Campaign spends three floors in each (approach, knot, seal) with rooms, pillars, keys, gates, and lore in the rail. Endless uses seeded rooms + corridors (same seed is the same crypt) and cycles the eight zones harder as the floor count rises.

Lore: the crypt is a lock, not a tomb. Four names were cut as teeth. The Drain keeps the ledger of what gets unnamed. Tithe rooms are the thief-god's joke. The last geometry is the door you hold or the door that holds you.

8. Multiplayer

Cabinet rules: everyone shares the camera and the floor. Food is contested. Vials are personal. A fallen warden can credit-in. Same-machine 1–4. Gamepads hot-join. Optional live lobby (same socket pattern as Lattice Golf) when the crypt lobby Space is up — room code, shared seed, ghost wardens.

9. Score

Foe + nexus + chest + floor clear bonus. Health remaining at exit is a small bonus. Credits used divide the posted score on the live hall. Local best stays in this browser.
