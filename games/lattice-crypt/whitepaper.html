Lattice Crypt v1.0

Four-warden co-op dungeon crawl in this browser. Pixel cabinets, eight realms, nexuses that never stop pouring, vials that clear a room, a Drain that only magic can end. Local 1–4 players, keyboards and gamepads. Radio. Live hall. No account. RESOURCE on the public page; dual ledgers / Star Chart stay CANON.

Play Arcade Ledger PayPal

1. Loop

You drop into a seeded maze. Health ticks down every second. Find the exit. Nexuses spawn foes until you smash them. Keys open doors. Rations buy time. Vials are smart bombs. Treasure rooms every eighth floor are a thirty-second grab. After eight realms (96 floors) the crypt loops harder. Score is the run. Insert a credit (Space / Start) when a warden falls to stand them back up at 700 health.

There is no last floor. The hall ranks score per credit, not floors cheesed with infinite continues.

4. Relics

  • Ration +100 health. Flask same, but a stray shot ruins it.
  • Blight looks like a flask — −100 and strips a buff.
  • Key opens one door. Doors also yawn open if the floor stays quiet long enough.
  • Chest score. Vial stored magic. Shooting a vial on the floor is a weaker room-clear.
  • Shot Codex / Swift / Aegis timed buffs (fire rate, speed, armor).
  • Veil foes lose the warden unless they bump. Reflect shots bounce once; bumpers take damage.
  • Pulse freezes nearby foes. Warp relocates the warden. Gates teleport between paired pads.
  • Trap tiles stun. Exit is the only way down, unless the floor stalls into a wall-exit after three minutes.

5. Spells (vials)

K / Magic / pad B: spend one vial. Damage in view = 20 × magic rank. Clears fodder, wounds elites, deletes the Drain. Orin clears rooms. Kael’s vial is a slap. Floor vials that get shot do half of that.

Hidden vials on some floors (banner: “A hidden vial waits”). Treasure rush floors (every 8th) are 30 seconds, extra chests, exit in a random corner. Layouts cycle: rooms, cross, cells, halls, arena, ring, vault, spiral.

6. Realms

Eight palettes, twelve floors each: Stone, Frost, Ember, Root, Tide, Gold, Void, Lattice. Seeded rooms + corridors. Same seed is the same crypt. After 96, the eight cycle with +rank on nexuses.

8. Multiplayer

Cabinet rules: everyone shares the camera and the floor. Food is contested. Vials are personal. A fallen warden can credit-in. Same-machine 1–4. Gamepads hot-join. Optional live lobby (same socket pattern as Lattice Golf) when the crypt lobby Space is up — room code, shared seed, ghost wardens.

9. Score

Foe + nexus + chest + floor clear bonus. Health remaining at exit is a small bonus. Credits used divide the posted score on the live hall. Local best stays in this browser.
