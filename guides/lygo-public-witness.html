Doctrine · August 2026

Public is reference. Lattice is canon.

By Justin Helmer · 27 August 2026

Two viral globes made the same point in different costumes. One rebuilt a government-looking news room from public RSS and local models. One made ADS-B, AIS, fires, and TLEs feel like a spy satellite. The replies that mattered were not “wow 3D.” They were: the visual layer is no longer a moat. The moat is data that never became public.

LYGO Public Witness takes the useful half of those ideas — a live Earth you can spin, labeled public layers, optional local Ollama — and refuses the dangerous half: treating a dashboard as intelligence it does not have.

The rule

  • If a fact never reached a public source, the globe stays empty there.
  • Public GET feeds (USGS earthquakes, NASA EONET, ISS) are REFERENCE.
  • Dual ledgers, kernel eggs, Agent Agora pulse, and the Haven Star Chart feed are CANON on-lattice.
  • Lattice-sphere placement is schematic. It is not a claim that an egg lives at that latitude.
  • This page never writes the live Star Chart.

How to use it
