Why this site exists

AI products change weekly. What does not change is the need for clear writing: how to brief an agent, how to hand work between sessions, how to avoid pasting secrets, and how to treat “done” as something you can re-check. chatagent.ca is our public notebook for those skills, tied to the LYGO project family without requiring you to install a full stack.

We keep a content-first homepage. Interactive tools live one click away. That is better for learners — and for quality programs that reject tool-only pages with thin text.
