WHAT IS *NOT* IN THIS INSTALLER (and why)
-----------------------------------------
No checkpoint ships here. The weights are the model author's work, they are
4-7 GB, and their licences are theirs — not ours to redistribute. get_model.ps1
downloads a checkpoint from Hugging Face on the user's behalf, from the author's
own repository, and reports which licence applies:

  sd_xl_turbo_1.0_fp16.safetensors   stabilityai/sdxl-turbo
      Stability AI Non-Commercial Research Community Licence.
      NON-COMMERCIAL. Research and personal use. Read it before selling output.

  v1-5-pruned-emaonly.safetensors    stable-diffusion-v1-5/stable-diffusion-v1-5
      CreativeML Open RAIL-M. Commercial use permitted with the use restrictions
      in that licence (including its attachment to derivatives).

Use -From <url> to point the fetcher at any checkpoint your own infrastructure
serves; whatever you point it at is your choice and your licence.

WHAT YOU MAKE IS YOURS
----------------------
Generated images are not restricted by this installer or by the LYGO kit. The
checkpoint's licence is the only thing that follows the output, and only when
you use that checkpoint. If you need clean commercial terms, use a checkpoint
whose licence grants them.

WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT
----------------------
Nothing. This engine runs on your machine and writes pictures into the console's
workspace\images folder. The web portal only reaches it when you start the
gateway yourself, and the gateway binds 127.0.0.1 by default (this PC only).
No telemetry, no accounts, no calls home.
