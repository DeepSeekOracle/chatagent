Good to know

What Signal is — and is not

Is: a free audio series about real published work by one independent maker. Stream it, download the MP3, mirror it, or run it on your own station.

Is not: journalism, a testimonial, or a paid promotion. The callers are AI-performed characters, not real listeners, and nothing in an episode is an endorsement of any product, model or service. Verify before you act on a claim.
